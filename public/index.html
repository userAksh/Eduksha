What's inside

Everything a curious kid & an involved parent need.

Eduksha is one app with a separate experience for the child and the parent — paired with a single PIN. Here's what they each get.

📝

Homework log

Snap, upload, and tag school homework. The day's pages are automatically organized into the Revisit-Past-Topics archive.

🧠

AI practice quizzes

Curated MCQs for every NCERT lesson, plus AI-generated practice from a kid's own uploaded notes. Score is tracked across attempts.

✍️

Written-answer feedback

Long-answer questions get gentle, model-evaluated feedback so kids learn how to structure responses — not just bubble in.

📰

Eduksha Times

A kid-safe daily newspaper of India-only motivational and knowledge stories — folds open like a real paper, refreshed every 24 hours.

🔥

Streaks & clans

Keep a daily learning streak going. Friends and parents see milestones; nudges show up at the right moments — never as guilt.

👨‍👧

Parent tasks

Parents assign chapters or custom challenges. Kids submit work. Parents review, accept, or ask for a redo — all inside the app.

How it works

Four steps. Then it's just a habit.

1

Parent signs up

Quick OTP login on the parent's phone. No social-media accounts required.

2

Add the child

Name, class, board. Generate a 4-digit PIN that the child uses on their device.

3

Child does the work

Homework, lessons, quizzes, and the daily newspaper — all in the kid-friendly tab.

4

Parent reviews

Push notifications when there's something to approve. One-tap acceptance keeps the streak alive.

Kid-safe by default

We took shortcuts on chrome. Never on safety.

Eduksha is built for children aged 8–13, with the stricter end of that range in mind. Here's the floor we hold ourselves to:

  • No advertising network. No tracking pixels. No social login.
  • News content is generated under a strict prompt: only motivational, educational, India-focused. Anything political, violent, or scary is filtered out before it reaches the device.
  • Every parent-side action requires the parent's authenticated session. Children can't escalate to parent mode without the parent PIN.
  • Photos and answers are encrypted in transit and stored in Google Cloud (Firestore + Cloud Storage), regions managed by Google.
  • Parents can request full deletion of their family's data at any time. Here's how.
