Kid-safe by default

We took shortcuts on chrome. Never on safety.

Eduksha is built for children aged 7–13, with the stricter end of that range in mind. Here's the floor we hold ourselves to.

  • No advertising network. No tracking pixels. No social login.
  • News is generated under a strict prompt: only motivational, educational, India-focused. Anything political, violent, or scary is filtered out before it reaches the device.
  • Children can't escalate to parent mode without the parent PIN.
  • Photos and answers are encrypted in transit and stored on Google Cloud (Firestore + Cloud Storage).
  • Parents can request full deletion of their family's data at any time. Here's how.

V — Frequently asked

Questions parents ask before downloading.

Is Eduksha really free?

Yes — the core experience (curated lessons, daily newspaper, AI quizzes, parent review, streaks) is free during the launch phase. We may introduce optional paid features later, but the current experience will always be available without payment.

What age group is Eduksha for?

Children aged 7–13, with content covering NCERT & CBSE classes 2 to 8. We're expanding curriculum coverage steadily — drop us an email if you'd like a particular class prioritised.

Do my child's homework photos leave the device?

Photos are uploaded to Google Cloud Storage so they show up in the Revisit-Past-Topics archive across devices. They are encrypted in transit and at rest. They are not shared with anyone outside the family group, and are never used for advertising. Full breakdown is in our Privacy Policy.

How do you keep the AI content kid-safe?

Every Gemini prompt — for the daily newspaper, quizzes, and written-answer feedback — runs through a strict child-safety filter that excludes politics, religion, violence, and distressing themes. We post-process outputs and audit them. If you ever see something off, mail team@eduksha.in — we investigate within 72 hours.

Can I use Eduksha for more than one child?

Absolutely. One parent account can hold multiple child profiles. Each child gets their own streak, progress, friends, and review queue — and you can switch between them from the parent dashboard.

Can a child use the same phone as the parent?

Yes. The parent stays signed in, and switching to the child experience requires the child's 4-digit PIN. Switching back to the parent view also requires re-authentication.

What if I want to delete everything?

You can delete your account from the in-app Profile tab, or by emailing team@eduksha.in. All photos, profiles, quiz attempts, and friend records are removed within 30 days. Backup snapshots purge within 90 days. See Delete account for the exact steps.
